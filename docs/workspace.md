# Workspace tools

The dashboard combines configurable widget instances with the available account modules. Layout, theme, wallpaper and interface language are account settings. Module access and external-service availability remain server-controlled.

Favorites provide personal shortcuts to accessible pages. Page locks reduce accidental editing and can have a temporary unlock; they do not replace sharing permissions or the secret vault.

The authenticator stores supported external TOTP entries under the application's protection. Keep independent recovery material for those external accounts. The application's own account 2FA and recovery codes are separate from these stored entries.

Split bills records supported expenses, participants, allocations, currencies, repayments and photos. The interface validates amounts and relationships before saving. Shared bill links are read-only and have their own access deadlines. Conflicting edits require a refreshed version rather than overwriting another change silently.

Use Notes, Reminders, Projects, Files and Messages for their respective records. Each module has its own deletion, retention and transfer rules. See [notes](notes.md), [sharing](sharing.md), [secret vault](secret-vault.md), [retention](data-retention.md) and [modules](modularity.md).
